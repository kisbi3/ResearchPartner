## Rules

Record decisions that affect scientific interpretation or reproducibility, including:

- changing assumptions
- choosing or rejecting a model
- changing parameter ranges
- waiving a baseline validation gate
- accepting a failed or partial validation
- including or excluding a result from a manuscript
- changing figure interpretation
- changing boundary conditions, initial conditions, units, nondimensionalization, random seeds, or integration schemes
